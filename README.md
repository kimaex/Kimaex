Kimaex

The Naira Gateway to Multichain DeFi

Kimaex is a Naira-first decentralized trading platform designed to make digital assets and decentralized finance accessible to users across Africa.

We are building a simpler way for users to interact with crypto: swap tokens, access decentralized trading, and experience blockchain technology through a familiar local-currency interface.

🚀 Our Vision

To make decentralized finance as accessible as traditional banking for millions of Africans.

💡 The Problem

Accessing decentralized finance can be difficult for everyday users because of:

- Complex wallet setups and blockchain interactions.
- Confusing gas fees and network switching.
- Dependence on foreign currencies such as USD.
- Fragmented trading experiences across multiple blockchains.

🛠️ Our Solution

Kimaex aims to provide a unified, Naira-first experience for decentralized finance.

Core Features

- Naira-first interface: A familiar Nigerian Naira-denominated experience.
- Multichain token swaps: Access digital assets across supported networks.
- Decentralized futures trading: Explore leveraged trading through integrated protocols.
- Crypto-to-fiat access: Simplify access between digital assets and local currency.
- Beginner and Pro modes: Interfaces designed for different levels of trad oking experience.

🌐 NEAR Protocol Integration

Kimaex is exploring NEAR Protocol infrastructure to make decentralized trading more seamless.

Our vision includes:

- Simplified blockchain interactions.
- Cross-chain asset accessibility.
- Reduced complexity for users.
- An intuitive gateway to decentralized financial applications.

🎯 Target Market

Our initial target markets are:

- Nigeria 🇳🇬
- India 🇮🇳

Our long-term vision is to expand across Africa and emerging markets.

🏗️ Technology

Our planned and evolving technology stack includes:

- Blockchain: NEAR Protocol, Solana, Base, BNB Chain
- Cross-chain infrastructure: NEAR Intents and other supported integrations
- Swap infrastructure: Jupiter and LI.FI
- Decentralized futures: Hyperliquid
- Frontend: Modern web application technologies

Note: Integrations and supported networks are subject to implementation and testing.
